  • Missouri Life Magazine: “75 years of photos”This state magazine included a story about the exhibit and its purpose. This story includes some photographs featured in the exhibit
  • Columbia Tribune: “Exhibit features images from 75 years of the Missouri Photo Workshop”This Columbia newspaper shared a few photographs featured in the exhibit.
