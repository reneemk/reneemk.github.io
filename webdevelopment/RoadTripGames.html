    <h3>Interstate Trivia</h3>

    <ul class="bullet-list">

        <li><strong>The Eisenhower Connection:</strong> Since you are starting in Abilene, KS, you are at the literal epicenter of the U.S. Interstate Highway System. President Dwight D. Eisenhower grew up there and signed the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1956, which built the very roads you are driving on!</li>

        <li><strong>The Great Divide:</strong> Somewhere along your path across the Rocky Mountain region, you will cross the Continental Divide. At that exact peak elevation, any drop of rain falling to the east flows to the Atlantic; any drop falling west goes to the Pacific.</li>

        <li><strong>The Volcano Peak:</strong> Mt. Rainier isn't just a mountain—it is an active stratovolcano! It is considered one of the most dangerous volcanoes in the world because of its massive amount of glacial ice, which could cause devastating mudflows (lahars) if it ever erupts.</li>

    </ul>
