🚗 Chapter 1: Shotgun Stand-Up (Jokes & Trivia)

Road Trip Groaners

  • Why did the ghost look for a hotel room in Abilene? Because it wanted a little rest-in-peace before heading west!
  • What kind of dinosaur loves a long car ride? A Prado-dactyl.
  • Why don’t mountains ever get cold in Washington? Because they wear snow-caps all year round!
  • What did Delaware? A New Jersey. What did Idaho? She-rad-a-baker (Studebaker) car, but you'll have to ask her when you cross the border!

Interstate Trivia

  • The Eisenhower Connection: Since you are starting in Abilene, KS, you are at the literal epicenter of the U.S. Interstate Highway System. President Dwight D. Eisenhower grew up there and signed the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1956, which built the very roads you are driving on!
  • The Great Divide: Somewhere along your path across the Rocky Mountain region, you will cross the Continental Divide. At that exact peak elevation, any drop of rain falling to the east flows to the Atlantic; any drop falling west goes to the Pacific.
  • The Volcano Peak: Mt. Rainier isn't just a mountain—it is an active stratovolcano! It is considered one of the most dangerous volcanoes in the world because of its massive amount of glacial ice, which could cause devastating mudflows (lahars) if it ever erupts.

🧐 Chapter 4: 20 Questions Game Prompts

One person picks a specific item/place/person within the chosen category. The rest of the passengers get up to 20 Yes/No questions to guess what it is!

  • The Highway Checklist: Something you can physically see outside the window right now (e.g., a specific mile marker, a windmill, a red semi-truck).
  • The Gas Station Snack Aisle: Anything you can buy to eat or drink at a rest stop.
  • Dwight D. Eisenhower's Closet: Things related to Abilene’s most famous resident or 1950s history.
  • The Packing List: Something currently inside the car, trunk, or glove box.
  • Volcanic Territory: Things associated with Mt. Rainier, magma, or glaciers.
  • Famous Washingtonians: Celebrities, musicians, or fictional characters from Seattle (e.g., Jimi Hendrix, Nirvana, Frasier Crane, or the Space Needle).
